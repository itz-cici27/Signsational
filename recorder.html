    Tips for good data
    • Record two takes per letter: one close to the camera, one farther away and slightly tilted.
    • Keep your whole hand in the frame. Plain backgrounds and good light help.
    • Every teammate should record every letter. More hands means a model that works on strangers' hands too.
    • The game uses samples in this browser right away. For the real model, download the CSV and run train.py.
