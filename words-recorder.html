    Tips for good data
    • Keep signing the word for the whole take. Each take is cut into about 10 short clips.
    • Record NONE too: wave, scratch your head, hold up random letters. It teaches the game what isn't a word.
    • Vary it between takes: closer, farther, faster, slower.
    • Download your CSV, put it in data/words/, and run python train_words.py.
